Questions people ask

Anything else, ask us on WhatsApp.

How much does an errand cost?

Every errand is priced on its own, based on distance, what the task involves, the number of stops and the size of what we carry. Send your order and we reply with one price before anything starts.

How do I pay?

Once you're happy with the price, we send payment details in the chat. The runner sets off after payment. For Buy & bring, the item budget is paid at the same time, and any change is returned to you.

What if an item isn't available?

The runner messages you before buying anything different. You choose a replacement or skip it. We never swap items without asking.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancelling before the runner sets off is free. After that, you pay for the trip already made and for any items already bought.

Is there a limit on what you'll carry?

We carry items worth up to ₦100,000 per errand, on a bike or on foot, so nothing bigger than a medium box. See the list above for what we won't carry at all.

Can I order for someone else?

Yes, including from outside Nigeria. Tick "Someone else will receive it" on the form and add their name and number. We keep both of you updated.
